 # Allegro Business Performance Dashboard

An independent business performance analysis of Allegro Group using Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.

*Analysis period:* Q1 2024 – Q2 2026  
*Data source:* Allegro Group official investor relations reports  
*Tools:* Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets

## Project Objective

Analyze Allegro Group's quarterly business performance, track key financial and operational KPIs, and identify trends in marketplace growth, revenue, profitability, and buyer activity.

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks five business KPIs:

- Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)
- Revenue
- Adjusted EBITDA
- Active Buyers
- GMV per Active Buyer (LTM)

Year-over-year (YoY) growth is calculated by comparing each quarter with the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

## Dashboard Overview

The Excel dashboard includes four visualizations:

1. GMV Trend
2. Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA Trend
3. Active Buyers vs GMV per Active Buyer (LTM)
4. Adjusted EBITDA by Segment: Poland vs International

## Key Insights — Q2 2026

| KPI | Q2 2026 | YoY Growth |
|---|---:|---:|
| GMV | PLN 19,571.8m | +14.38% |
| Revenue | PLN 3,339.5m | +16.13% |
| Adjusted EBITDA | PLN 1,030.1m | +11.54% |
| Active Buyers | 20.9m | +1.64% |
| GMV per Active Buyer (LTM) | PLN 3,528.2 | +9.77% |

### Business Insights

- GMV increased by 14.38% YoY, indicating continued marketplace growth.
- Revenue increased by 16.13% YoY, outpacing GMV growth.
- Adjusted EBITDA grew by 11.54% YoY, but at a slower rate than revenue.
- Active Buyers increased by 1.64% YoY.
- GMV per Active Buyer (LTM) increased by 9.77%, suggesting higher transaction value per buyer contributed to growth.
